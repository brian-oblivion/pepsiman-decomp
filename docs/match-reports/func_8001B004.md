## How it went

func_8001A69C's body with the colour word replaced by a depth-cue sequence
was one word short and had two stack slots swapped:

- The colour as a `u32` local landed after the two `s32` locals; as a
  `CVECTOR` filled through `*(u32 *)&c` it takes the 8-byte slot right after
  `sv[4]` (sp+0x20), with `v` and `dp` at 0x28/0x2C, as retail.
- The packet code byte store (`sb 0x2C, 7(p)`) went into the following
  `beqz`'s delay slot, which then saved retail's `nop` after `lb`. A
  volatile store, `((volatile POLY_FT4 *)p)->code = 0x2C;`, stays out of the
  slot and gives the exact schedule.

### Proposed learning

- A store retail keeps above a branch that the build moves into the branch's
  delay slot (retail then has `li` in the slot and a load-delay `nop` later):
  make the store volatile; reorg does not put it in a slot.
