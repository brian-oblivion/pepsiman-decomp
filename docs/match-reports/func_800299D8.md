# func_800299D8 — MATCHED 295/295 (round 9, charlie)

Unit `src/code_1a098.c`. The floor query: walks the faces of one zone of
the floor data (`D_800958B4` in its callers), skips faces with a zero
flag, tests `pos` (x and z) against each edge of a triangle (`v[3] ==
0xFFFF`) or a quad, and on the first face that contains it takes the
normal (OuterProduct0 of two edges), sets `out->y` to the plane's height
at `pos`, the normalised normal and the face's flag, and returns the
height. No face: 0x7FFF; a vertical face: 0.
