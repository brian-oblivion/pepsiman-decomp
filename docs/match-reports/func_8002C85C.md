### Proposed learning

An array element's address summed into the BASE's register rather than
the index's (`addu $v0,$idx,$base` with `$v0` holding the base): the source
loaded the base into a pointer local and advanced it, `p = tbl; p += i;`.
`tbl[i]` and `&tbl[i]` give the index's register. (func_8002C85C)
