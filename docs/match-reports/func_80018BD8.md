# func_80018BD8 — STALL (gp seam): 9 words short, 7/51, first diff at word 6 (+0x18, the first D_800956D0 access)

Unit `src/code_7d74.c`, parked under `#ifdef NON_MATCHING` with the
`INCLUDE_ASM` in its `#else`. Round 5, runner charlie.

Steps a signed level byte `D_800956D0` up on flag 0x10 and down on flag
0x80 of the flag word `D_80095970`, clamps it to 0..120, and on flag 0x20
writes 0xFF to byte 8 of the game state head, sets `D_800956D1` and copies
the level into byte 6.

**The C is retail's.** The only difference is how `D_800956D0` is reached:
every one of its nine accesses here (and the one in func_80017B38) is
absolute (`lui $v0` / `lbu $v0, %lo(...)`, stores through `lui $at`), while
`main` (asm/nonmatchings/main/main.s, `sb $zero, %gp_rel(D_800956D0)($gp)`
at 0x800132B4) reaches it through `$gp`. `config/gp-symbols.txt` lists it
(generated by `tools/gpsyms.py` from that `main` access), so maspsx makes
every symbolic access gp-relative and each `lui` disappears: 9 words short.
This is the "source-file seam" in docs/LEARNINGS.md: one gp list for the
whole build, so no C spelling closes it. It is a segmentation or toolchain
decision for the head, not something a runner can fix.
