## Why it stalls: the packet's code-byte store and loop strength reduction

Every instruction matches retail's except the packet-side register roles.
`cc1 -dL` (the loop dump, run on the unit in the scratch directory; the
toolchain untouched) shows why: loop records the code-byte store
(`sb` at `pkt+7`) as a DEST_ADDR giv of the packet biv and combines it into
the reduced giv of the last packet address (`pkt+0x14`, the third colour,
here; `pkt+0x1C`, the third UV, in func_80021434), so cc1 writes
`sb -13(giv)` / `sb -21(giv)`, keeps the giv in `$a2` and copies the packet
pointer to a fresh register (`move $t0/$t1, $a2`, the extra word). Retail
reduces the same giv but leaves the code-byte store on the biv (`sb 7($a2)`),
so the pointer never moves out of `$a2`.

Source shapes tried (each a full oracle build or a `-dL` check of the giv
table), none of which keeps the `sb` out of the combination:
`pkt->code`, `((u8 *)pkt)[7]`, `((P_TAG *)pkt)->code`, a `volatile u8`
store, libgpu's `setcode`/`setShadeTex`, the store moved after the OT
linking, the store through a `CVECTOR *` local that also feeds `gte_strgb`,
a per-iteration `p = pkt` copy, and `prim[i]` indexing (worse: drops the
`prim+3` giv retail has). With the store placed after the UV stores, the
`sb` becomes the absorbing giv instead, also wrong.

The permuter could not be set up: tools/setup-permuter.sh fails assembling
the target, `unrecognized opcode rtpt/nclip/avsz3/dpct` (posted to the
broadcast; not debugged, CLAUDE.md hard rule 6).

The siblings func_80021240 (G4) and the other gouraud/textured handlers
show the same pattern (the last packet address reduced, `sb 7($a2)` on the
biv), so whoever cracks this shape gets several functions.

About 9 full builds and a dozen loop dumps; no build improved on the first
body's register-only diff. Stopped at the round's eight-function budget.
