### Proposed learning

A block move with a dead unaligned (`lwl`/`lwr`) loop jumped over: a copy
of a byte-aligned struct (`u8 b[N]`) whose source alignment cc1 cannot
see, such as through a pointer local. Word copies with no such loop: an
`s32`-array wrapper. (func_8002C2B4)
