sprite 0x136 slides in from the left (frames 0..15, 15 pixels a frame),
holds at x = -72 while its blue/alpha byte blinks down in an 8-frame cycle
(16..31), then slides out to the right (32..47). Drawn with code_a0bc's
func_8001B354 into the current ordering table; returns 1 at frame 48.

## Levers

- **The colour's first byte is a real `s8` member.** With `CVECTOR` and the
  unit's usual `*(s8 *)&color.r = -1;` the build was one word short: in the
  middle phase cc1 sank the `-1` store below the `lh` of `p->unk0` and used
  it to fill the load delay (retail: `sb r; sb g; lh; nop`). The store
  order in source did not matter (r/g swapped gave the same output). A
  local `SColor { s8 r; u8 g, b, cd; }` passed as `(CVECTOR *)&color`
  keeps the store above the load and matches. The cast form is still right
  for func_8003C17C and func_8003FFAC, where no load follows.
- Each phase makes its own call; cross-jumping shares the tail
  (`D_800ACEA8[D_80095750]` and the `jal`) the way retail does.

### Proposed learning

- **An `s8` -1 byte store that retail keeps above a following load: a local
  struct whose member is declared `s8`**, not a `*(s8 *)&` cast on a
  `u_char` member; the cast's store can be scheduled past the load.
  (func_8003EF40)
