## The residue: `D_80095830` is a scalar

Retail: `lui $v1, %hi(D_80095830)` / `lbu $v1, %lo(D_80095830)($v1)`.
Built: `lui $v0` / `lbu $v1, ...($v0)`. Those are the only two differing
words.

All 57 loads of `D_80095830` in `asm/` use one register for both the
`lui` and the `lbu`: the shape the assembler's macro expansion of a
symbolic load gives (it borrows the destination register). cc1 splits
the address of an array of unknown size into its own pseudo, which the
allocator puts in another register. Measured: with `extern u8
D_80095830;` in `common.h` (and `mode = D_80095830;`) the function is
18/18 and the whole image is OK. A unit-local `extern u8
D_80095830[1];` after `common.h`'s declaration does not help (cc1
classifies the symbol at its first declaration; still 16/18).

### Proposed learning

- **One register for both halves of every `lui`/load pair of a global:
  a scalar, not an array.** When every retail site of a symbol loads
  with `lui $X` / `l? $X, %lo(sym)($X)`, it is a symbolic scalar load
  expanded by the assembler; an array of unknown size gives cc1's split
  into two registers wherever the allocator separates them.
- **A literal `== 1`, `< 2`, `== 0` chain on a byte: nested `if`s on an
  `s32` copy.** The `switch` and the `&&` forms fold. (func_800414EC)
